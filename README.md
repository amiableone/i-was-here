# 📍 I Was Here

A tiny Telegram bot that lets people leave a mark — like signing a guestbook.

## How it works

1. Press **I was here** — you're recorded as the latest visitor
2. Optionally leave a short message
3. Anyone who opens the bot sees who was last here and what they said

<!-- Screenshot: bot showing last visitor with profile link and message -->

## Commands

- `/start` — see who was last here
- `I was here` — mark your visit
- `Skip` — save your visit without a message
